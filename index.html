Hello! I’m Sofia Colocho, a student and aspiring developer from El Salvador. I enjoy learning new technologies, discovering different cultures, and taking on challenges that support my academic and professional growth.

At 17, I discovered that technology was more than machines; it was a canvas for turning ideas into real-world solutions. What began as curiosity in front of a screen became a passion for understanding how a few lines of code could come to life.

I chose this path to build rather than simply consume. Seeing the challenges around me helped me realize that working with technology gives me the ability to shape the future instead of waiting for it to happen.

Featured Projects

Learning experiences where I have applied technology to address real-world needs.

Power Apps

FurryLove

FurryLove is a project focused on helping dogs and cats find a loving home, through a mobile and web application. The project’s main objective is to simplify the adoption process for those interested in adopting pets responsibly. The project was created to reduce the time animals spend in shelters and to promote responsible pet adoption. It also aims to raise awareness about animal care and support shelters by giving visibility to animals in need of a home.

Website

ColorMagico

ColorMagico is a website designed for children, with the goal of making the process of learning colors simpler, more interactive, and more fun, thereby enhancing the learning experience. Recognizing an opportunity to engage with children, our team created this site by integrating an artificial intelligence tool called Teachable Machine; it uses a camera to identify the color being shown and, once the analysis is complete, provides an accuracy percentage.

Web Platform

CityRide

City Ride is a transport application that allows users to access shared private transport services to get home affordably. The platform addresses the lack of regular public transport, which often stops operating at a certain time, by enabling users to check available routes to their destinations via the app. Users can pay in cash or through digital payment methods, and by sharing the ride with others heading to the same area, they benefit from a lower cost.

Website

ContaFacil

ContaFácil is a financial website dedicated to entrepreneurs who want to have better control over their business. Many entrepreneurs struggle to keep track of expenses, income, sales, and inventory, making it difficult to understand their actual profitability. For this website, we identified the needs of small businesses and designed a way to record financial activity, including sales, expenses, and products.
